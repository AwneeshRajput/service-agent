# Transmission and Clutch

Internal reference for service advisors.

**Scope:** we service transmission fluid and diagnose faults. We do not rebuild
gearboxes or replace clutches in house. Those go to a specialist.

## Automatic transmissions

| Symptom | Likely meaning |
|---|---|
| Harsh or jolting shifts | Fluid degraded, or worn internal clutch packs |
| Engine revs climb but speed does not | Slipping — serious, stop driving |
| Long pause before drive engages | Low fluid pressure, worn pump |
| Shudder at steady light throttle | Torque converter lock-up clutch |
| Whine that changes with road speed | Bearing wear inside the unit |
| Locked in one gear | Limp mode protecting the transmission |
| Burnt smell from the fluid | Overheated and past its life |

Slipping is the one to act on. A transmission that slips is grinding its own friction
material into the fluid, and every further kilometre makes the repair more expensive.

## Manual transmissions

| Symptom | Likely meaning |
|---|---|
| Revs rise without acceleration, worst uphill | Clutch slipping |
| Bite point very high or very low | Clutch worn, or hydraulic fault |
| Crunch selecting gears, especially second | Worn synchromesh, or clutch not releasing |
| Pedal stays on the floor | Failed master or slave cylinder |
| Rumble that stops when the clutch is pressed | Release bearing |

## The job we do

- **TRN — transmission fluid change, 60 min.** Drain and refill to the correct level
  at the correct temperature, which for many automatics is a specific procedure rather
  than simply filling to a mark.

On "sealed for life" units: that phrase means the maker did not schedule a change, not
that the fluid lasts forever. On a car used for towing, heavy traffic, or one past
100,000 km, a fluid change is usually worthwhile. Be honest that on a neglected, already
slipping transmission, fresh fluid will not undo wear and occasionally makes shifts feel
worse.

## Electric vehicles

Most EVs have a single-speed reduction gearbox with no clutch and no conventional
transmission fluid service. Shift-quality complaints on an EV are usually motor control
or drive software, not mechanical — book a diagnostic and see
[ev-high-voltage.md](ev-high-voltage.md).

## Escalation

Slipping, internal noise, metal debris in the fluid, or any clutch replacement is a
specialist job. Book **DIA** to confirm the diagnosis, document it, and refer with the
findings so the customer does not pay twice for the same investigation.
