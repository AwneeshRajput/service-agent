# Air Conditioning and Climate Control

Internal reference for service advisors.

## What customers report

| Symptom | Usual cause |
|---|---|
| Blows cool but not cold | Refrigerant low because of a leak |
| Cold at speed, warm in traffic | Condenser blocked with debris, or a cooling fan fault |
| Weak airflow at every setting | Cabin filter clogged |
| Musty smell on start-up | Bacteria on a damp evaporator |
| Loud click or squeal when A/C switches on | Compressor clutch or belt |
| Windows mist up and clear slowly | A/C not running; it is the dehumidifier, not just the cooler |
| Water pooling in the footwell | Blocked evaporator drain, not a leak from outside |

## The most important thing to explain

Refrigerant is not consumed. A sealed system holds its charge for years. If it needs
regassing, it leaked, and it will leak again. We therefore always lead with the
diagnostic rather than a regas.

## The jobs

- **ACD — diagnostic, 45 min.** Pressure readings both sides, vent temperature, and a
  UV dye or sniffer leak test. This is the honest first step whenever cooling is weak.
- **ACR — regas, 60 min.** Evacuate, vacuum test to confirm the system holds, then
  recharge to the exact weight the manufacturer specifies. The vacuum test is what
  separates a real service from simply topping up a leak.
- **CAB — cabin filter, 20 min.** First thing to check for weak airflow or smell.
  Cheap, quick, and often the whole answer.

Common leak points are the condenser at the front of the car, where stones hit it, and
the compressor shaft seal. A leaking condenser is a replacement, not a repair, and needs
a quote rather than a fixed price.

## Seasonal advice

Run the A/C for ten minutes a month, even in winter. The compressor seals rely on the
oil that circulates with the refrigerant, and a system left unused all winter is the
one that fails in the first hot week.

## Electric vehicles

Most EVs use a heat pump that both heats and cools, and the same circuit often cools
the traction battery. That means an A/C fault on an EV can reduce charging speed or
range, so treat it as more than a comfort issue. Work involving the battery cooling
loop is high-voltage adjacent: see [ev-high-voltage.md](ev-high-voltage.md).

## Escalation

Systems that have been opened to the atmosphere for a long time, or that have had
sealant products poured in, may need parts we do not stock. Book the diagnostic and
tell the customer a quote will follow.
