# Oil Changes and Scheduled Servicing

Internal reference for service advisors.

## What is included

- **OIL — oil and filter change, 45 min.** Semi-synthetic oil to the grade the
  manufacturer specifies, new filter, new sump plug washer, level check, and the
  service reminder reset.
- **SYN — full synthetic oil change, 45 min.** Same work, better oil. Required on most
  turbocharged and newer engines, and worth it for short-journey driving.
- **BSV — basic service, 90 min.** Oil and filter, plus a check over of fluids, lights,
  tyres, brakes and wipers, with a written report.
- **MSV — major service, 180 min.** Basic service plus air filter, cabin filter, spark
  plugs where due, and a fuller inspection.

Related items customers often add: **AIR** engine air filter (20 min), **CAB** cabin
filter (20 min), **CLT** coolant flush (60 min), **TRN** transmission fluid (60 min).

## Intervals

Follow the manufacturer's schedule. Where the customer does not know it, a reasonable
default is annually or every 15,000 km, whichever comes first.

Shorten that when the car does mostly short trips, frequent cold starts, heavy towing,
or a lot of idling in traffic. Oil degrades by time and contamination as much as by
distance, which is why a low-mileage car still needs an annual change.

## Reading the symptoms

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Oil dark on the dipstick | Normal. Detergents hold soot in suspension; colour is not a test |
| Level below minimum | Top up now and investigate. Some consumption is normal, a lot is not |
| Ticking or tapping at cold start | Oil not reaching the top of the engine quickly — often overdue |
| Blue smoke on start-up | Oil passing valve stem seals |
| Red oil pressure light | Stop immediately. Continuing will destroy the engine within minutes |
| Milky residue under the oil cap | Condensation on a short-trip car, or coolant mixing — needs investigation |

The oil pressure light is the one to be blunt about. It is not a reminder that a service
is due; it means oil is not circulating under pressure.

## Electric vehicles

EVs have no engine oil, so OIL, SYN, AIR, SPK and TRN do not apply. They still need
brake fluid, coolant for the battery circuit, cabin filters and tyre work, and they
still benefit from a scheduled inspection. If a customer with an EV asks about an oil
change, explain the reduction gearbox is usually sealed for life and check what the
maker actually specifies.

## Escalation

An engine that has run with no oil pressure, or that is consuming more than a litre
every 1,000 km, needs investigation beyond a service. Book a diagnostic and flag it.
