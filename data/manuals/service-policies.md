# Booking, Pricing and Warranty Policies

Internal reference for service advisors. These are the rules the assistant should
follow when answering customers.

## Opening hours

- Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00, at every branch.
- Saturday, 09:00 to 13:00, at Seattle Downtown, Denver Central, Austin North and
  Chicago Loop only. Portland East and Atlanta Midtown are closed on Saturdays.
- Closed Sundays and on public holidays.

All times are local to the branch. A customer in one time zone booking at a branch in
another should always be told times in the branch's local time.

## Booking

- Work is booked into a bay for a whole number of hours. A job estimated at 90 minutes
  occupies two hourly slots, so a three-hour job cannot start at 16:00.
- Longer jobs must start early enough to finish before closing.
- We hold the booking for 15 minutes past the appointment time. After that the bay may
  be released to another customer.
- Same-day slots are often available; check rather than assume.

## Cancellation and changes

- Cancel or move a booking free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time.
- Inside 24 hours we may charge a late-cancellation fee.
- Repeated no-shows may mean future bookings require a deposit.
- Customers can rebook to any branch, not only the one they used before.

## Prices and quotes

- The price quoted at the time of booking is the price honoured, even if our list price
  changes before the appointment.
- Prices differ by branch and by vehicle class. A quote for an SUV does not apply to a
  hatchback, and a quote for one city does not apply in another.
- Never quote a firm price for work that depends on inspection — brakes, suspension,
  diagnostics. Quote the inspection, then the repair once it is known.
- Where a promotional discount applies, state both the normal price and the discounted
  price so the saving is visible.
- Any part we have to order is quoted separately and may add a day or two.

## Payment

- Payment is due on collection.
- We will always call before carrying out work beyond what was booked. No additional
  work goes ahead without the customer agreeing first.

## Warranty

- Parts we supply and fit are covered for 12 months or 20,000 km, whichever comes first.
- Labour on a job we carried out is covered for 12 months.
- Warranty does not cover wear items consumed in normal use, damage from an accident,
  or faults caused by parts the customer supplied.
- Bring the invoice, or give us the booking reference and we can find it.

## Vehicles we cannot take

- Vehicles that are unsafe to drive should be recovered, not driven in. We do not
  provide recovery.
- Accident damage, high-voltage battery damage, and internal engine or gearbox rebuilds
  go to a specialist. We can diagnose and refer.

## When to hand over to a person

The assistant should stop and offer a human advisor when the customer:

- Disputes a charge, or asks for a refund or compensation.
- Reports an injury, a fire, or a safety incident.
- Asks about work we have not quoted and that needs judgement.
- Is clearly distressed, or asks for a person.

Never guess at a price, a date, or a diagnosis to fill a gap. Saying "I will check that
with an advisor" is always better than an answer that turns out to be wrong.
