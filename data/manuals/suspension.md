# Suspension and Steering

Internal reference for service advisors.

**Important for booking:** we do not have a fixed-price suspension job. Suspension work
is quoted after inspection. Book the customer in for an alignment (ALN) or a diagnostic
(DIA) as the inspection slot, then raise a quote for parts and labour.

## What customers report

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Knocking or clonking over bumps | Worn anti-roll bar links, or top mounts |
| Car keeps bouncing after a bump | Shock absorbers past their useful life |
| Nose dives heavily under braking | Front shocks worn |
| Clunk when turning at low speed | Ball joint or top mount |
| Steering feels vague or wanders | Track rod ends, worn bushes, or alignment |
| Creaking over speed bumps | Dry or perished bushes |
| Uneven, cupped tyre wear | Shocks no longer controlling the wheel |
| Car sits lower on one corner | Broken coil spring |

## Quick checks

- **Bounce test.** Push down hard on one corner and release. It should settle within
  one rebound. Repeated bouncing means the damper is worn.
- **Visual.** Oil film running down a shock absorber body means it has lost its damping
  fluid and is finished.
- **Bushes.** Cracked or missing rubber, or a bush that has split away from its sleeve.
- **Springs.** A broken coil often shows as a short, squared-off end where it should
  be a smooth curve.
- **Boots and gaiters.** Split gaiters let grit into the joint and turn a cheap part
  into an expensive one.

## Why it matters beyond comfort

Worn dampers lengthen braking distances, because a wheel that is bouncing is not fully
in contact with the road. A customer who thinks they are living with a slightly bumpy
ride is also living with worse braking and faster tyre wear.

Parts are replaced in axle pairs. Fitting one new shock on a car with three tired ones
gives uneven handling.

## Ordering

Suspension components are rarely stocked. Expect to order against the vehicle
identification number, and allow a day or two before booking the fitting slot.

## Electric vehicles

EVs carry significant battery weight low in the structure, so springs and bushes are
under more load than the petrol equivalent, and replacement parts are usually specific
to the model. Never fit a component intended for the combustion version of the same car.

## Escalation

Anything involving steering rack internals, subframes, or accident damage goes to a
specialist. Document what you found, quote the inspection only, and refer.
